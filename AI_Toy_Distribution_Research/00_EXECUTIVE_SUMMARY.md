## TL;DR Strategic Findings

1. **The closest direct competitor (Embodied Moxie) is dead.** Moxie shut down Nov 26, 2024 ($799 launch, later $499–$1,500). Existing units on $29.99/mo Sunlight cloud. The $149–$349 AI companion slot is open — and the displaced Moxie customer base is actively looking for replacements.
2. **Don't pursue Medicare/Medicaid DME coverage** for the toy. CMS Article A52469 excludes general-purpose consumer electronics from SGD coverage; the toy fails the "dedicated device" and "primarily medical purpose" tests. Pursue private insurance gaps and family grants instead.
3. **UHCCF grants ($5K/yr, $10K lifetime per child, any commercial insurance)** are the most direct family-funding lever for a $149–$349 hardware purchase. Provide a "How to submit for UHCCF" one-pager.
4. **IDEA Part B (school-side, IEP-funded)** is the legitimate path for special-ed / adaptive-need classroom purchases. The toy should be marketed as IEP-supportable AT, targeting special-ed directors and AT specialists.
5. **Kaplan Early Learning is the only educational distributor with publicly documented wholesale terms:** NET60, no MOQ, **15% off list, free UPS shipping on $100+**, via cooperative contracts (Iowa bid portal, NDESC, Purchasing Connection #25.2-KAP, TIPS, TexBuy). Use cooperative contracts to bypass individual MOQ negotiations.
6. **K-12 sales cycle is 6–18 months; pilot-to-contract is typically a year** (EdWeek, NationGraph). Start in October–March to land on the April–June budget cycle.
7. **Classroom-pack pricing model:** Lexia is the benchmark — $20–$50/seat, $5K–$12K school site license. Target $25–$40/seat or $4,000/30-device classroom bundle.
8. **Subscription layer ($5–10/mo or $99/yr) aligns with the market norm** — Miko Max is $99/yr, CoughDrop is $200/yr. Plan for recurring revenue on top of hardware margin.
9. **The Pre-K AI tutor market has a gap at the hardware tier.** Khan Academy Kids is free; Khanmigo is software-only ($4/mo individual, ~$10/student/yr district). ABCmouse, HOMER, LeapFrog Academy do not publish school pricing. No widely-adopted $149–$349 hardware AI tutor in the Pre-K classroom slot was found.
10. **Trade shows to attend (Oct 2025 – Dec 2026):** ATIA (Jan 29–31, 2026, Orlando), Toy Fair NY (Feb 14–17, 2026), CSUN ATC (March 2026, Anaheim), ABAI (May 2026), ASTRA Marketplace (Jun 5–8, 2026, Milwaukee), ISTELive + ASCD (Jun 28 – Jul 1, 2026, Orlando), Closing the Gap (Oct 20–22, 2026, Minneapolis), ASHA Convention (Nov 2026, location TBA).

## Channel Priority Recommendations

### Primary (fastest revenue)
1. **DTC / Amazon / own site** — consumer purchase; UHCCF grant guides as supporting content.
2. **Special-ed / IEP-funded district sales** — AT specialist + special-ed director. Long cycle (12–18 mo) but high LTV.
3. **Clinical endorsement channel** — BCBA + SLP recommendations drive parent pull. Don't gate this on insurance reimbursement.

### Secondary (longer-lead, lower revenue)
4. **Educational distributor (Kaplan first, then cooperative contract vehicles)** — once you have the cooperative contract, districts can buy without a separate RFP.
5. **Hospital pediatric units / child life specialists** — ACLP conference + Child's Play charity introductions; ACLP credentialing; gatekeeper is the Child Life Specialist (CCLS).
6. **ABA therapy clinics** — pitch as RBT-efficiency / engagement tool (CPT 97153 is time-billable, so the toy only adds margin if it improves session outcomes).

### Do NOT pursue
- Medicare/Medicaid DME coverage as a primary channel.
- General-purpose CES retail outside specialty stores.

## Honest Caveats

- **No published industry statistic** for "% of ABA sessions involving screen-based tech." Estimates exist but not authoritative — don't cite a number.
- **State-by-state HCBS waiver AT caps** vary; AT3 Center is the aggregator but state plan documents need direct review.
- **Khanmigo district pricing** is in a $10–$90/student/yr range based on case-study leaks; the official pricing page requires a sales conversation.
- **School Specialty, Really Good Stuff, Lakeshore, Discount School Supply** do not publish wholesale MOQs/payment terms publicly. Contact each for specifics.
