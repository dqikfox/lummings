# Lummings — Brand Rules One-Pager

**Pin this. Follow this. Do not redesign the cast.**

## 1. Style lock
- Look = **Cinematic Nest / Little Tomorrow Crew** (fluffy, soft eyes, one prop, Heartlight glow).
- Match: `brand/character-masters/`, `crew_hero`, `app/assets/images/crew/`.
- **Do not** change looks, silhouettes, or invent a new art style.

## 2. Heartlight
- **Essence / soul of that Lumming** — **NOT** a separate creature.
- Names: Questlight · Dreamlight · Traillight · Tidelight · Sparklight.

**Lumo · Lila · Pip · Nori · Moki**
