# VISUAL LOCK — The Lummings / Little Tomorrow Crew

**Status:** LOCKED. Do not generate, redesign, or substitute character art.
**Date:** 19 October 2026
**Audience:** Every human and every AI working on this project.

## Absolute rules

1. **Do not generate new character images.**
   - No ChatGPT, Midjourney, Flux, ComfyUI, or any other model output of Lumo, Lila, Pip, Nori, or Moki.
   - No "updated" versions, no "cleaner" versions, no "in the style of".
   - The existing production portraits are the final look.

2. **Use only the canonical files listed below.**
   - If a file is not in the Canonical list, do not use it for characters.
   - Old Luna / Sprout files, early concept art, and any previous AI generations are retired.

3. **Names are locked:**
   - Public and product names: **Lumo · Lila · Pip · Nori · Moki**
   - Luna and Sprout are retired names. Do not use them in new work.

4. **If you need a new asset** (expression, pose, icon, scene):
   - Reference the canonical portraits exactly.
   - Match silhouette, colour, prop, and fur style.
   - Do not invent a new design.
   - Prefer existing SVGs in `docs/portraits/` and `app/assets/images/portraits/` over generating.
