# Privacy policy: The Lummings bedtime app

*Last updated 27 October 2026. This is a plain-language summary of what the app does today. It has not been reviewed by a lawyer.*

## The short version

- **No account. No login.** The app asks for no name, email or age.
- **Your child's progress stays on your device.** It is saved in the browser. We do not receive it.
- **No ads. No tracking scripts. No analytics.** Your browser loads nothing from other companies' servers, including fonts. (One thing happens on our server: see "Voices".)
- **The microphone is off by default.** A grown-up can switch on "Talk". If they do, read the microphone section below.
- **Voices** are mostly recordings that ship with the app. If a line has no recording yet, our server asks ElevenLabs to say it in that friend's voice. Only the friends' own story lines are sent: never your child's name, memories or anything they say. See "Voices".

## What the app saves, and where

Everything below is saved **only in your browser on your device**, under one entry named `lummings`. It never leaves your device through the app.

- which friend your child chose, and the bond level and care streak
- short memories typed in the app
- the grown-up settings: the gentle time limit, whether the microphone is off, whether to skip silly choices, and mute
- today's usage time (to apply the gentle limit)
- the parent PIN (four digits)

**About the PIN:** it is a gentle gate on a shared phone, not strong security. It is stored on the device and someone who can open the device's browser tools could read it. Do not reuse a PIN you use anywhere else.

## Voices

Most of what the friends say is a recording that ships with the app. If a line has no recording yet, the app asks **our own server** to turn that one line into speech in the friend's voice using **ElevenLabs**, a text-to-speech company. The text sent is only the friends' own pre-written story lines and choices. It never includes anything your child types or says, their memories, or any name. ElevenLabs receives our server's request, not your device's address. The app never uses your browser's built-in voice, and it does not clone anyone's voice.

## The microphone and "Talk"

"Talk" is **off by default** and can only be switched on from the grown-up screens. If a grown-up turns it on, the app asks the browser's built-in speech recognition to listen. Browsers such as Chrome on Android may send that audio to the browser maker's speech service to turn it into text. **We do not receive or store that audio**, but we cannot control what the browser's speech service does with it. The app shows a notice when you switch Talk on. Leave it off if you do not want this.

## What our website and hosting see

When you load the app or website, the hosting provider (the company that serves the files) may record your IP address and basic request details in its server logs, as any web host does. We do not use those logs to identify children and do not combine them with anything else.

## The waitlist (adults only)

The website has an optional waitlist form. If an adult enters an email address, it is sent to a small waitlist service run by the maintainer, which stores the address so we can tell you about early access. We do not sell or share it and do not use it for anything else. To have your address removed, open an issue at <https://github.com/dqikfox/lummings/issues> (do not post the address publicly; say you want it removed and we will arrange a private way to confirm).

## Children

The app is made for children together with a grown-up. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, and the waitlist is for adults. Because progress lives only on the device, there is nothing for us to show or delete on our side.

## Deleting data

On the grown-up screen, **Clear the bond** (enter the PIN) erases the saved progress on that device. You can also clear this site's data in your browser settings, which removes everything the app saved.

## Open source

The code is public at <https://github.com/dqikfox/lummings> so anyone can check what it does. The names and character art are not covered by the open-source licence.

## Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change. To ask a question or report a concern, open an issue at <https://github.com/dqikfox/lummings/issues>.

*The maintainer is based in Sydney, Australia.*
