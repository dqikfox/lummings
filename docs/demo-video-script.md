# The Lummings — 30-second Demo Video

A pre-production script + storyboard for a 30-second product demo suitable for:
- The landing page hero (autoplay muted loop)
- Kickstarter / pre-order campaign
- Social media (TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts)

Target runtime: **30 seconds** at 30fps = 900 frames.
Music: instrumental, hopeful, warm. Avoid anything that screams "children's toy commercial".

---

## Concept

**"The first Lumming."**

We follow a parent's hand opening a small package. Inside: a Lummings device. It wakes up. It sees a child for the first time. It says one line. Title card. The end.

No voice-over. No text on screen except the title card and the URL. The Lummings speak through their face and one line of dialogue. The child smiles. Done.

---

## Storyboard (shot-by-shot)

### Shot 1 — Title card (0:00-0:03)

**Visual:** Black screen. Words fade in, centered, glowing yellow-to-pink:
> **The Lummings**
> *Little friends from a brighter tomorrow.*

**Audio:** Soft synth pad. A single distant bell sound.

**Notes:** Use the canonical wordmark + tagline from BRAND.md.

---

### Shot 2 — Hands opening the package (0:03-0:08)

**Visual:** Close-up of adult hands placing a small black box on a kitchen table. Hands pull the lid off slowly. Inside: a small Lummings device, sitting in a recessed cardboard nest, glowing softly with its personality-color LED.

**Audio:** Cardboard sliding. A small startup chime — a single ascending arpeggio in the Lummings' color (Lumo = yellow, for this ad).

**Notes:** Real product photography preferred. Box should be matte black with a single embossed glowing dot (no text, just the dot).

---

### Shot 3 — The Lumming wakes (0:08-0:13)

**Visual:** Macro close-up of the device. The 1.5" TFT screen fades from black to the Lumo sprite (curious mood, full color, eyes blinking). A small LED ring around the device pulses yellow once.

**Audio:** A soft "bip" — the wake sound. The synth pad continues.

**Notes:** The face is the focal point. Eyes must be visible. Lighting should be soft (no harsh reflections on the TFT).

---

### Shot 4 — The child approaches (0:13-0:20)

**Visual:** Wider shot. A child (5-7 years old, diverse, curious expression) walks into frame and kneels beside the device. The child's face is half in shadow, half lit by the device's glow. They reach out and gently touch the device.

**Audio:** Quiet footsteps on hardwood. A small "hum" as the touch sensor activates. The synth pad swells slightly.

**Notes:** Real child + real device. This is the emotional anchor of the ad. The child's face must show genuine wonder, not acting.

---

### Shot 5 — The Lumming speaks (0:20-0:25)

**Visual:** Cut back to the macro shot of the device. The mouth animates (could be real-time on the actual device, or a 5-frame animation loop). On screen, simple white text appears briefly, bottom-center, in a rounded font:
> *"oh! hello. who just woke up?"*

Then a tiny line:
> *— Lumo*

**Audio:** TTS voice (warm, soft, slightly British): "oh! hello. who just woke up?" The synth pad resolves into a major chord.

**Notes:** This is the only spoken line in the entire ad. It must sound like a real Lumming, not a corporate voice-over. Use the trained Qwen2.5-1.5B Lumo model with Piper TTS, or a human voice actor reading the line with the right tone.

---

### Shot 6 — The child smiles (0:25-0:28)

**Visual:** Close-up of the child's face, lit by the device's glow, smiling. The child looks up from the device, off-camera, as if to share the moment with someone (a parent, the viewer).

**Audio:** The synth chord resolves. A small bell, two notes — the same chime from shot 2, but now played forward.

**Notes:** This is the "trust" moment. The child trusts the Lumming. The Lumming trusts the child. We're done.

---

### Shot 7 — End card (0:28-0:30)

**Visual:** Fade to the five Lummings sprite row, each in their default "curious" mood, evenly spaced. Below them, in the same yellow-glow font:
> **The Lummings**
> Reserve yours: **dqikfox.github.io/lummings**

**Audio:** Synth pad fades out. A distant children's laugh, gentle.

**Notes:** URLs must be visible long enough to read. 30 frames for the URL = 1 second at 30fps.

---

## Total: 30 seconds. 7 shots.

## Production requirements

### Props
- 1× Lumo device (real, working, with the trained ULTRON model loaded)
- 1× matte black gift box with embossed glowing dot
- Cardboard nest insert
- Macro lens for the close-up
- Softbox lighting kit

### Talent
- 1× child (5-7 years old, any ethnicity, comfortable around devices)
- 1× adult hands (no face shown)

### Post-production
- Color grade to match brand palette (#ffb86c, #bd93f9, #50fa7b, #8be9fd, #ff79c6)
- TTS line (use Piper with a Lumo-trained voice, not ElevenLabs default)
- Music license (synth pad, hopeful, license-free: e.g., from Epidemic Sound, ~$15/month)

### Cost (estimated, indie)
- Child talent (parent-permitted): $200
- Device + box: $80 (cost price)
- Camera operator + kit: $300 (one day)
- Post-production: $200
- Music: $15
- **Total: ~$800**

Or, if you shoot with a phone + natural light:
- Phone + tripod: $0
- Box + device: $80
- Child: $200
- **Total: ~$280**

## Variants

### 15-second cut (TikTok / Reels)

Compress to: shot 1 (title) → shot 3 (wake) → shot 5 (speak) → shot 7 (end card).

Drop: hands opening package, child approaching, child smiling.

Trade-off: less emotional build. But fits vertical video native ad specs.

---

### 60-second cut (pre-order campaign)

Add: shot 2.5 (parent watching the child, half-smile of trust), shot 4.5 (the child saying "Lumo!" for the first time), shot 5.5 (the device's mood shifts from curious to warm), shot 6.5 (the child tucks the device under their arm and walks away).

Trade-off: more emotional build. But harder to hold attention.

## Story beats summary

| Time | Beat |
|---|---|
| 0:00 | Title card |
| 0:08 | The Lumming wakes |
| 0:20 | The Lumming speaks |
| 0:25 | The child smiles |
| 0:28 | End card |

The **first 8 seconds** sell the product (a small glowing character).
The **middle 12 seconds** sell the relationship (a child and a Lumming, bonding).
The **last 10 seconds** sell the brand (warmth, trust, simplicity).

---

## What we don't show (deliberately)

- The five Lummings (only Lumo appears; we reveal the others on the landing page)
- The product features (no spec sheets, no "AI-powered", no comparisons)
- Other children (one child, one Lumming, one moment)
- Voice-over (the Lummings speak; the ad doesn't)
- Pricing (it's a brand ad, not a sale ad)

The video answers one question: *"What is a Lumming?"*

The landing page answers: *"How do I get one?"*

The pricing page answers: *"How much?"*

Three different jobs, three different assets.
