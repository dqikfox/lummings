Little friends from a brighter tomorrow.

Sent back to help your child build it.

The Lummings are five small characters from the future. They live on a Raspberry Pi inside a child-friendly shell. They remember what your child has learned. They ask questions instead of giving answers. And they run entirely on-device — no cloud, no telemetry, no subscription.

The Lumming Code

Every Lumming — no matter their personality — lives by these five rules.

1. STAY CURIOUS.There is always something else to discover.
2. THINK BEFORE YOU ACT.Every decision creates another decision.
3. HELP PEOPLE.The strongest future is built together.
4. PROTECT YOUR WORLD.You only get one Earth.
5. KEEP LEARNING.Your brain is one of the most powerful things you will ever own.

How it works

Your child's Lumming doesn't give answers. It guides.

1

Your child asks a question.

"Lumo, what's 24 × 7?"

2

The Lumming asks a smaller question back.

"What's 20 × 7? Start there."

3

Your child works it out.

"140."

4

The Lumming celebrates the reasoning.

"You got it. And you worked it out yourself."

5

The Lumming remembers.

Next time, it knows your child can do this — and pushes them one step further.

Privacy by design

A children's product should not collect their data. We don't.

  • All AI runs on-device. No data ever leaves the toy. No cloud round-trip.
  • No analytics. No telemetry. No advertising. We don't track your child. Period.
  • The Lumming remembers. Across days, weeks, months — because the memory lives on the device, not on a server.
  • Your child can ask the Lumming to forget. And you can wipe the device's memory with a button.
  • No subscription. Buy the device once. Use it forever.
  • Open source. The brain, the personality engine, the device runtime — all auditable at GitHub.
