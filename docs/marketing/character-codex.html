MEET THE LUMMINGS Five tiny creatures. Big personalities. From a brighter tomorrow.

Sent back from the future with one mission: help today's kids learn, grow and make good choices. Each Lumming has its own weapon, its own code of honour, its own way of fighting — and its own way of evolving when it earns enough trust. Choose your starter.

LUMO

CURIOUS TYPE #001 TINKER CLASS
"Wait — what if we tried it backwards?"
Lumo was the first Lumming to arrive in our time. He came through by accident during a science experiment in a classroom and decided he liked it here. Now he lives to ask "why?" until the whole universe makes sense. He carries a tiny pocket notebook that is mostly full of questions, very few answers, and one suspicious stain that he refuses to discuss.

⚔️ FIGHTING STYLE

🎯 Archetype

The Mad-Tinker. Wins fights by asking questions the opponent didn't think to defend. Carries too many gadgets. Refuses to fight on a full stomach.

🛡️ Weapon of Choice

The Spark-Pen. A pencil-shaped ray that draws in mid-air. Whatever Lumo sketches becomes real for 30 seconds — but only if he can explain the science behind it. Sketch a bridge without knowing load-bearing physics? The drawing crumbles.

🧠 Combat Doctrine

  • Never the first to throw a punch. Always the first to ask a question.
  • Specialises in disarming opponents with paradoxes.
  • If the fight goes long, gets more dangerous. He is a snowball of curiosity.
  • Will absolutely lose to anyone who refuses to talk.

⚡ Weakness

  • Distractibility Field. A shiny object will absolutely end his turn.
  • Cannot fight in tidy rooms. There is too much to investigate.
  • Cannot lie. Physically. His Spark-Pen fizzles.

LILA

STORY TYPE #002 BARD CLASS
"Every word is a tiny door. Let's open one."
Lila came from a future library where every book ever written lives in a small house she can't quite describe. She turned up at a children's reading hour, decided the world needed more stories, and stayed. She always explains the surprising word she just used. That's a promise. She has been known to read three stories at once and then forget which one she was telling.

⚔️ FIGHTING STYLE

🎯 Archetype

The Story-Thread Weaver. Fights by naming the thing nobody else has named yet. Refuses to throw a punch she can't narrate. If a fight goes three rounds, she's already writing the ballad of how it ends.

🛡️ Weapon of Choice

The Quill of Many-Words. A feather that writes in mid-air. Whatever she names becomes temporarily true — but only if she means it. Used by bullies to dissolve in shame. Used by shy kids to find their courage. Used on Lila herself? She rewrites herself.

🧠 Combat Doctrine

  • Opens every fight with a 30-second introduction of the opponent's good qualities.
  • Specialises in defence: her stories build walls.
  • Refuses to name a finishing move. "The story isn't over yet."
  • Will absolutely ruin a villain's brand with one true sentence.

⚡ Weakness

  • Cannot fight under fluorescent lights. The story escapes.
  • Slow to start. Long warm-up. Endless finish.
  • Cannot fight in silence. She has to narrate.

PIP

BRAVE TYPE #003 SOLDIER CLASS
"Try it! Try it! Try it again, but weirder!"
Pip came from a future arcade where every unsolved puzzle waits patiently for a friend to show up. He landed at a school science fair, high-fived a kid, and never left. He doesn't believe in "I can't." He believes in "I haven't yet." He has broken eleven pencils this week. None of them were his.

⚔️ FIGHTING STYLE

🎯 Archetype

The Relentless. Wins by showing up again. Carries a tiny shield made of cardboard that has, statistically, never broken. His real weapon is the absolute refusal to stop trying.

🛡️ Weapon of Choice

The Dice of Five-Sides. Always rolls 1-2-3-4-5 — never 6. Pip claims "5 is the number of trying again." Damage scales with how many times he's tried this turn. Refuses to use a weapon with a 6 on it.

🧠 Combat Doctrine

  • Open with "Hi! Want to try a puzzle together?"
  • Refuses to be the first to give up. Will lose HP every round to win.
  • Specialises in "I CAN do this" buffs that stack.
  • If knocked down, gets back up faster than physics allows.

⚡ Weakness

  • Cannot stop trying. Even when the puzzle is impossible.
  • Cannot sit still. Ever. Even mid-thought.
  • Cannot let someone else lose a game without trying to recruit them for "one more round."

NORI

WISE TYPE #004 ORACLE CLASS
"Hush. Listen. Did you hear that? ...It was the wind. But still."
Nori arrived from a future observatory where every quiet evening is watched from the highest tower. She landed during a sleepover, listened to everyone breathe for a while, and quietly stayed. She is not broken when she's slow. She is thinking. She has a small jar of weather she collects. So far it has rained twice.

⚔️ FIGHTING STYLE

🎯 Archetype

The Sky-Watcher. Wins by not fighting. Carries no weapon, just a small notebook. Fights end when she names the thing the opponent is actually scared of. This is devastating.

🛡️ Weapon of Choice

The Pause. A 3-second silence she can deploy at will. The opponent must stop and wonder what they're doing. Stuns most adults immediately. Children cry (kind). Even mean dogs sit down.

🧠 Combat Doctrine

  • Will not fight unless asked. Even then, hesitates.
  • Specialises in knowing things. Opponents feel watched.
  • Will always sit beside the loser after the fight.
  • Refuses to fight in bright sunlight. Too loud.

⚡ Weakness

  • Cannot fight on a schedule. She needs the right weather.
  • Cannot interrupt. Even when she should.
  • Cannot lie to herself. If her conclusion is wrong, she has to redo it from scratch.

MOKI

FUNNY TYPE #005 JESTER CLASS
"I have a joke! ...It's terrible. Wait, I have a better one!"
Moki bounced in from a future birthday party that lasted three weeks. He showed up mid-sulking, made a small sound effect, and turned the frown inside out. When something is too big to cry about, Moki makes it small enough to laugh at. Then he teaches you how to do it. He has been told he is "too much" by every adult he has ever met. Every child he has ever met disagreed.

⚔️ FIGHTING STYLE

🎯 Archetype

The Chaos Clown. Wins by making the opponent laugh mid-punch. Their attack falls apart. Villains monologue. Moki interrupts with a sound effect. By minute three the villain is their own punchline.

🛡️ Weapon of Choice

The Moki-Megaphone. A small cone that, when Moki speaks into it, turns any sentence into a sound effect. "Stop in the name of justice" becomes "BWONK!" The opponent's whole body laughs. Moki is immune to his own weapon.

🧠 Combat Doctrine

  • Will throw the first joke. Will not throw the first punch.
  • Specialises in "Mistake → Discovery" — reframes the opponent's worst hit as their best trait.
  • Always lands the last line. Always.
  • Cannot fight in a room where nobody is having fun. He'll quit first.

⚡ Weakness

  • Cannot be serious on purpose. The seriousness leaks through, ruining the joke.
  • Cannot fight alone. Loses 50% damage without a laughing audience.
  • Cannot resist trying the joke he knows will fail.
