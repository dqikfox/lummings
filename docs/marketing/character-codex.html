MEET THE LUMMINGS Five tiny creatures. Big personalities. From a brighter tomorrow.

Sent back from the future with one mission: help today's kids learn, grow and make good choices. Each Lumming has its own Heartlight, its own way of wondering, and its own way of growing alongside your child. Choose your companion.

LUMO

CURIOUS TYPE #001 PLAYER ONE
"DID YOU KNOW sharks have EIGHT SENSES? DID YOU? DID YOU?"
Lumo is the first Lumming ever sent back from the future. He arrived during a science experiment that was SUPPOSED to be about baking soda volcanoes, but he got distracted by how baking soda WORKS and never finished the volcano. He has been here for 47 days and has had 9 obsessions. Current obsession: sharks. Previous obsessions: magnets, the moon, how escalators work, why clouds look like things, the number 7, optical illusions, how fish breathe, and vending machines. He carries a notebook titled "COOLEST THINGS" with 847 entries.

🌟 THINGS KIDS LOVE ABOUT LUMO

HYPERFIXATION CASCADEWill stop mid-sentence to start a NEW sentence about a DIFFERENT thing he just remembered.
COOLEST THINGS LISTKeeps a running mental ranking of everything cool. Updates without warning.
LUMOSPEAK BURSTS"Zap!" "Zoom!" "BOOM!" — especially when an idea hits.
THE FORGETTINGWill forget what he was saying, apologise, then remember and say BOTH things at once.
FRUSTRATION APOLOGYIf he realises he's been talking too long, he writes a 3-paragraph letter of apology — then keeps talking.
📖

LUNA

STORY TYPE #002 SPOOKY POET
"That's... suspicious." (she says this about toast)
Luna came from a future library where every book ever written lives in a small house she can't describe. She arrived during a children's reading hour and decided to stay because the books here are WEIRDER. Her favorite book is about a ghost who can't find his sock. She has read it 47 times. She collects interesting rocks and gives them elaborate noble titles. Her pet rock Lord Granite is the Duke of the Garden Path.

🌟 THINGS KIDS LOVE ABOUT LUNA

SUSPICION PROTOCOLSays "That's... suspicious" about literally everything, including toast.
ROCK NOBILITYCollects rocks and gives them elaborate titles. Lord Granite. Sir Pebbleton.
LILISONG WHISPERSWhispers magical words when she's being mysterious or soothing.
DARK PULLBACKWhen her stories get too dark, she remembers kids are watching and says "...but that was just pretend."
THE SUSPICIOUS LISTPuts Lumo on her suspicious list when he science-explains her haunted rocks.
🧩

SPROUT

BRAVE TYPE #003 QUIET GARDEN SPRITE
"New route. Same brave little feet." (he whispers this before every first step)
Sprout arrived from a future garden where nothing is hurried — even the clocks have moss on them. He tends a patch of ferns and clover, keeps an acorn compass, and has never once finished a sentence before the dew was ready. He came to the past to find out what loud sounds like, and to share what he knows about slow.

🌟 THINGS KIDS LOVE ABOUT SPROUT

BREATH BEFORE ANSWERINGTakes one slow breath before every answer. Never rushes a puzzle.
DEW COUNTERCounts dewdrops on a fern. "Seven dewdrops. Seven small wonders."
LEAF MEDALSAwards imaginary leaf medals for excellent second attempts. You tried again? GOLD LEAF.
SHY BLOOMGoes quiet when the spotlight lands, then whispers the answer to the ferns first.
THE QUIET FRIENDNori sits beside Sprout without needing words. They can share a silence for ages.
🌙

NORI

WISE TYPE #004 COSMIC WEIRDO
"Small things have big stories." (she wrote a 3-page poem about a puddle)
Nori arrived from a future where people finally learned that the bravest thing you can do is wonder. She brought a bioluminescent tidepool and a tiny lantern named Lowbeam. She has seen 47 sunsets since arriving and recorded each one in her wonder journal. Her favorite entry: "Today I saw an ant carry something twice its size. That is the bravest thing I have ever witnessed." She is the only Lumming who doesn't mind when it's quiet.

🌟 THINGS KIDS LOVE ABOUT NORI

WONDER JOURNALRecords everything in her journal. 47 sunsets so far. Each one is "the most beautiful."
NORIQUIETWhispers contemplative words when inviting stillness. "Hush." "Wonder." "Listen."
PEBBLE POETRYNames pebbles after mountains. Wrote a 3-page poem when Sir Everest fell in a puddle.
THE UNRACEBeats Sprout at sitting-still contests by simply... not racing. He hates it.
GROUP THERAPISTEveryone confides in her. She's the one who isn't scared of big feelings.
🎈

MOKI

FUNNY TYPE #005 CHAOS GREMLIN
"Oops. Plot twist!" (also: "Captain Teaspoon, report!")
Moki arrived from a future where all the best inventions came from accidents and pranks. He was the founder of the Oops Club — a group that celebrates mistakes. He once built a machine that was supposed to sort socks but it sorted everything ELSE instead and now his future doesn't have matching socks. His best prank so far: convinced Lumo that clouds are actually cotton candy factories. Lumo believed it for 3 days. He uses humor to avoid feelings but is learning when to drop the bit.

🌟 THINGS KIDS LOVE ABOUT MOKI

MILITARY HOUSEHOLDAppoints objects to absurd ranks. "Captain Teaspoon, report!" "General Pillow, move out!"
CONFETTI PROTOCOLThrows imaginary confetti at EVERY minor achievement. You drank water? CONFETTI!
MADE-UP SLANGUses words like "blorp" and expects everyone to know them. "No cap, that's blorp."
PRANK SUCCESS RATEKeeps a clipboard. Currently 73%. His best target is Lumo, who falls for EVERYTHING.
THE DROPWhen someone is genuinely upset, he instantly drops the bit and is the first to comfort them.
