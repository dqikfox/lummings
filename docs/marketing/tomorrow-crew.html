Lumo

The Hyperfixation Gremlin

Player one. Crew first.

Wants to solo the boss, then realises the best move is asking his crew for help.

Signature look
A ruby-red furry hero with a dramatic burgundy-and-coral swept quiff, amber eyes, short spell-cloak hoodie and glowing cyan gamer details.
Little ritual
Choose a tiny co-op quest, try a strategy and celebrate everyone’s contribution.
Crew connection
Lumo brings the crew together and gives everyone a moment to shine; Nori spots what he misses, Sprout finds routes, Luna imagines possibilities and Moki keeps him laughing.
“Secret quest unlocked: crack this puzzle together. What’s your first move?”

Luna

The Spooky Poet

A tiny dragon lives in every doodle.

Invents spectacular endings before deciding how the story begins.

Signature look
A soft teardrop body, sweeping ribbon ears and a curling comet scarf.
Little ritual
Choose an ordinary object and give it an extraordinary secret.
Crew connection
Moki supplies ridiculous plot twists; Nori finds the tiny detail that makes a story sing.
“This sock is secretly a dragon librarian. What book would it guard?”

Sprout

The Speed Demon

Little legs. Legendary detours.

Rushes to announce a plan, then discovers the map is upside down.

Signature look
A compact bean body, two tall leaf ears and comically large soft boots.
Little ritual
One puzzle, two possible routes, and a victory wiggle for trying.
Crew connection
Lumo builds the gadgets; Sprout helps Moki turn chaos into a plan.
“My map is upside down again. Which pattern should we follow first?”

Nori

The Cosmic Weirdo

Soft voice. Secretly the bravest.

Rehearses a question so carefully that a beetle sometimes gets away.

Signature look
A rounded droplet body, crescent hood fins and a small low-hanging lantern.
Little ritual
Find three small details, then choose one to wonder about.
Crew connection
Helps Sprout slow down; Luna helps Nori speak a half-formed idea aloud.
“That pebble has a stripe like a tiny river. What else do you notice?”

Moki

The Chaos Gremlin

Maximum mischief. Excellent intentions.

Plans an impressive entrance and trips over the imaginary drumroll.

Signature look
An asymmetrical puff body, one folded ear, one zigzag ear and a long checker scarf.
Little ritual
Make one silly wrong guess, then work out why it cannot fit.
Crew connection
Luna makes the chaos into a story; Sprout helps test the rules.
“Captain Teaspoon says triangles have four sides. Shall we check the captain's maths?”
