Privacy

Short version: The Lummings is built to keep children's data on the device. There is no account, no subscription, and no cloud service watching what a child says.

Last updated: 24 October 2026

What we collect

  • Nothing by default. The web app runs locally in the browser. Conversations and drawings are processed on the device that created them.
  • No accounts. There is no sign-up, no email capture, and no profile.
  • No behavioural tracking. We do not run third-party analytics, ad pixels, or fingerprinting scripts.

Payments

Asset packs are sold through Stripe. Stripe handles the payment details; we receive the transaction record (name, email, amount) needed to deliver your purchase and meet tax obligations. Card numbers never touch our systems.

Third parties

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, and payments run through Stripe. Both process the minimum data required to serve the page and complete the transaction. Optional purchases may use the PIKA.ME platform for image generation, subject to their own terms.

Children

The Lummings is designed for children aged 5–12 with a parent or guardian in the loop. The characters are safety-first by design: they ask before they act, and they never surface unsafe content. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, and we do not run targeted advertising of any kind.

Your rights

Because we hold almost no personal data, there is very little to request, export, or erase. If you have an order with us and would like it removed, email dqikst@gmail.com and we will action it.

Contact
