# Lummings family app campaign

Prepared 13 October 2026. Creative draft for adult-facing promotion; no ads have been purchased or published.

## Positioning

**Small friends. Very big what-ifs.**

Five wonderfully imperfect companions turn a short screen activity into something a family can talk about, make, or investigate together. Promote the current authored browser experience: five branching adventures, local family profiles, a creative notebook, and parent controls. Do not advertise live AI, cloud sync, improved grades, enforced parental security, subscriptions, or hardware as features of this version.

Destination: `docs/companion/for-parents.html`. Replace local preview URLs with the verified published URL before running a campaign.

## Three ready-to-use ad concepts

### 1. The dinner-table question

Headline: **A little screen time. A bigger dinner conversation.**

Body: Meet Lummings: five curious companions, branching adventures, and little discoveries to bring into your world. Count stars. Invent a story. Test a wonderfully wonky idea together. Try the free browser app—no sign-up needed.

Visual: Existing parent-and-child illustration alongside an actual adventure screenshot. Label illustrative artwork where needed.

CTA: **Find your family’s first adventure.**

### 2. The imperfect captain

Headline: **He has a magical controller. He still gets the maths wrong.**

Body: Lumo is learning something important: the best move is asking his crew. Join him at the Constellation Gate, choose a strategy, and discover why checking a guess can be an adventure.

Visual: Ruby-red Lumo portrait with the real star-counting board.

CTA: **Meet your co-op captain.**

### 3. The paper hammock

Headline: **The bridge collapsed. The curiosity didn’t.**

Body: Moki calls it a paper hammock. You might call it a lovely chance to try again. Lummings adventures connect playful stories with small things to make, notice, and explore together.

Visual: Moki portrait beside a simple paper bridge; do not imply an actual customer endorsement.

CTA: **Try a little what-if.**

## A 20-second video script

- 0–4s: Lumo portrait, then the star puzzle. “Some questions need a little friend.”
- 4–9s: Luna’s story choices and the creative notebook. “Some ideas need a wonderfully weird beginning.”
- 9–14s: Moki and a real paper-bridge demonstration. “And some adventures are just getting started when the screen goes off.”
- 14–20s: Five portraits and actual app home. “Lummings. Small friends. Very big what-ifs. Try an adventure together.”

Use captions throughout. Keep the invitation directed to grown-ups. Avoid urgency, social exclusion, purchase pressure, or simulated affection conditioned on spending.

## Review before distribution

Compare these concepts with adult volunteers using the same destination page. Ask which promise they understood, whether the app delivered it, and what confused them. This app contains no campaign analytics. Collect any adult feedback separately with clear permission; do not add child profiling to measure campaign performance. Pricing, paid plans, and public publication remain separate launch decisions.
