# Indie Hackers post

**Title:** $0 MRR, 1 self-order, 8 live SKUs — my honest first 30 days solo-launching a 3-product shop

**Body:**

Three weeks ago I launched a 3-product shop. Today:

- 8 paid SKUs ($0-$499)
- 1 paid order (mine)
- 4 newsletter signups
- 3 free-lead captures
- Real Stripe checkout live
- Telegram bot handling orders
- 6 gh-pages landing pages
- 10 demo assets generated

Revenue: $0.

Stack: Python FastAPI on a homelab box, gh-pages for storefront, Stripe for payments, Telegram bot for orders, sub-$50/mo infra.

Three lessons I want to highlight:

1. **Build the distribution before the product.** I built the shop first, the audience second. That's the wrong order. The honest second is to start with an audience (Reddit, X, Substack) and then build what they want.

2. **Free lead magnets work.** I added a CC0 10-asset pack, a 5-portrait pack, and a SHInEyVErSE image sampler. Three downloads turned into three leads in one test. That's a 100% conversion from lead magnet to captured email.

3. **One product > three products.** Claude (yes, I'm a Claude user too) gave me this advice in a second-opinion review. I led with the wrong product. Now I'm leading with The Lummings (kids' AI) and demoting the others.

What's next:

- 5-7 quality Reddit posts (not 30)
- itch.io page
- Show HN submission
- One Indie Hackers post (this)

Will report back in 30 days with what actually moved the needle.

If you're shipping solo and stuck at $0, the thing that worked for me was building the smallest possible thing that delivers real value, then iterating. Free is fine. Local-first is a real positioning.
