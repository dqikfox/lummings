**Title:** The Lummings — 5 local-AI characters for kids

**Short description:** Five distinct personalities. Persistent memory. Local-only AI. Ages 5-12. Free.

**Full description:**

The Lummings are five local-AI characters for kids aged 5-12. Each has a distinct personality:

- Lumo (yellow) — the curious one, asks "why?" about everything
- Lila (teal) — the creative one, sees stories in everything
- Pip (green) — the energetic one, always running, always trying
- Nori (blue) — the thoughtful one, listens carefully before answering
- Moki (magenta) — the mischievous one, tells you when your joke is bad

Everything runs on your device. The AI model (quantized 7B), the conversation memory, the character engines — all on your hardware. No cloud. No telemetry. No monthly subscription.

**Why itch.io?** This is a free web app. Itch.io is the indie home for free games and toys. Real discovery traffic.
