Little friends from a brighter tomorrow

Lumo, Luna, Sprout, Nori, and Moki.

Five bedtime friends. They tell a long story, play a small game in the middle, and stay quiet when they have no recording. The bond stays on this device.

Lumo

Hyperfixation Gremlin · Questlight

Player one, crew first. Lumo pauses the quest if you need a breath.

Luna

Spooky Poet · Dreamlight

She keeps the pencil until you are ready for the next page.

Sprout

Quiet Garden Sprite · Traillight

The trail can wait. Sprout walks it with you, slowly.

Nori

Cosmic Weirdo · Tidelight

Small questions get a small, steady answer. Then rest.

Moki

Chaos Gremlin · Sparklight

A kind glitch. The sparkles turn down to a night-light.

A night, not a gadget pitch

Open a story. It is twelve pages. Two of them are games: roll a die, count, guess, or spot the one that does not match. Then the book closes.

1. Pick a friend Their own story comes first. The night the crew met comes after.
2. Turn the pages Nothing hurries. A wrong game answer only means try again.
3. A grown-up gate Screen time, a PIN, and a wipe of the bond live behind it.
4. The toy can wait These five already live in the bedtime app. A shell comes later, if a family actually uses them.

The five lights

Questlight Lumo. Crew first.
Dreamlight Luna. The page can wait.
Traillight Sprout. The path stays.
Tidelight Nori. The moon keeps watch.
Sparklight Moki. Soft, then quieter.

What stays here

A children’s page should not collect them.

No account The bond is saved in the browser on that device.
No microphone for the stories If a line has no recording, it stays silent.
No ads The extra page is not for sale. The story is the product.
